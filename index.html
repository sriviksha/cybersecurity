Stay Alert. Stay Safe.

Learn to recognize online scams and misinformation.

🎣 Phishing Scams

Scammers use fake emails, messages, or websites to steal personal information.

Safety tips

Check website addresses. Never share your password or OTP with anyone.

📱 Digital Arrest Scams

Fraudsters impersonate police or government officials and threaten people through calls.

Safety tips

Do not panic or transfer money because of a threatening call. Verify independently.

🤖 AI Fake News

AI-generated text, images, audio, and videos can be used to spread misinformation.

Safety tips

Check the original source, publication date, and trusted news organizations.
